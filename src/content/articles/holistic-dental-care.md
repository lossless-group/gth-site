When we ran her oral microbiome test, everything became clear.

Harmful bacteria had colonized her mouth, creating systemic inflammation that was affecting her energy, sleep, and immune function.

Within three months of addressing the root cause through holistic dental care, her gums stopped bleeding, her energy returned, and she finally understood what her body had been trying to tell her all along.

## **Where traditional dental training leaves a gap**

For the past 18 years as a functional dentist, I've watched thousands of patients trapped in the same frustrating cycle.

They brush twice daily, floss religiously, get their professional cleanings — and still end up with bleeding gums, bad breath, cavities, and exhaustion their doctors can't explain.

This isn't because their dentists aren't skilled or caring. Most dental professionals are incredibly dedicated.

The issue is that ***traditional dental education focuses primarily on treating disease after it appears***, rather than investigating the systemic factors that caused it in the first place.

Here's what's often missing from the conversation: ***your mouth is the gateway to your entire body****. *

You swallow between 150 billion and 1 trillion bacteria every single day.¹

When those bacteria are out of balance, it doesn't just affect your teeth — it affects your heart, brain, gut, and immune system.²⁻⁵

Standard dental protocols effectively address the immediate problem — they remove decay, restore function, and relieve pain. ***These are essential skills. ***

But without understanding the [oral-systemic connection](/blog/oral-systemic-connection) and the role of the oral microbiome, the underlying imbalance often remains, leading to recurring issues.

This gap in approach made sense decades ago when we thought bacteria were simply "bad" and needed to be eliminated with antibiotics and aggressive mouthwash.

But modern science has revealed a more nuanced picture.

We now know that beneficial oral bacteria are critical to your longevity, and that ***indiscriminately destroying your oral microbiome can actually make you more susceptible to the diseases*** we're trying to prevent.

## **What makes holistic dental care different**

Holistic dental care — also called functional dentistry or biological dentistry — builds upon traditional dental training ***with additional focus on the oral-systemic connection***.

Instead of only asking "what's wrong with your teeth," I also ask *"what's happening in your body that's showing up in your mouth?"*

This integrative oral health approach incorporates three principles that expand beyond conventional dental practice:

**First, your mouth and body are one interconnected system.**

The same bacteria causing your bleeding gums can travel to your heart and brain.²⁻⁵

Research published in *Science Advances* found harmful oral bacteria in the brains of Alzheimer's patients, with levels directly correlated to cognitive decline.²

The oral-systemic connection isn't theoretical — ***it's measurable and undeniable***.

## **The emerging science of oral health and chronic disease**

Here's what the latest research reveals: [***oral bacteria don't stay in your mouth***](/blog/oral-health-and-chronic-disease)***.***

Every time you chew, brush, or floss, you create tiny openings that allow bacteria to enter your bloodstream.⁵

This is an area where dental and medical research are increasingly converging.

Studies show that oral bacteria have been found in most of atherosclerotic plaque specimens analyzed.³

When harmful strains like *Fusobacterium nucleatum* and *Porphyromonas gingivalis* enter your circulation, they trigger systemic inflammation that contributes to:

- [**Heart disease and stroke**](/blog/oral-health-and-heart-disease) - Research confirms patients with gum disease are more likely to experience heart attacks.⁹
- **Diabetes complications** - The relationship is bidirectional: gum disease worsens blood sugar control, and high blood sugar feeds harmful bacteria.⁴
- [**Cognitive decline and dementia**](/blog/gum-disease-and-dementia) - That 2019 landmark study found gum disease bacteria in Alzheimer's patients' brain tissue, suggesting a causal role.²
- **Chronic inflammation** - Your immune system fighting oral infections diverts resources from healing elsewhere in your body.⁵

The mouth-body connection works both ways.

When Jennifer came to our program complaining of constant [brain fog](/blog/brain-fog-causes) and digestive issues alongside her dental problems, [oral microbiome testing](/blog/oral-microbiome-testing) revealed the same harmful bacteria colonizing both her mouth and gut.

We addressed both simultaneously, and within weeks her mental clarity returned.

## **When to consider a functional dental approach**

You might benefit from holistic dental care if you're experiencing:

- Chronic [bleeding gums](/blog/bleeding-gums) despite regular cleanings.
- Recurring cavities even with excellent hygiene.
- Persistent bad breath that won't go away.
- Dry mouth *(which significantly increases cavity risk⁶).*
- Diagnosed chronic conditions like diabetes, heart disease, or autoimmune disorders.
- Unexplained fatigue, inflammation, or brain fog.
- Concerns about mercury fillings or dental materials.

Here's something that often surprises people: ***dry mouth significantly increases cavity risk.⁶***

Saliva contains antimicrobial properties and helps maintain proper pH balance.⁷⁻⁸

When saliva flow decreases — whether from medications, stress, or other factors — harmful bacteria flourish unchecked.⁶⁻⁷

While most dental offices recommend staying hydrated, biological dentistry takes it further by investigating *why* saliva production has decreased and creating comprehensive protocols to restore it.

This deeper investigation often reveals connections to medications, systemic health conditions, or nutritional deficiencies that can be addressed.

## **The testing-first philosophy that changes everything**

In my practice, I never start treatment without data.

Guessing doesn't work when you're trying to optimize a complex ecosystem housing up to 700 different bacterial species.

Oral microbiome testing uses cutting-edge bacterial DNA sequencing to identify exactly which strains are present in your mouth and in what quantities.

This reveals:

- Which harmful bacteria are driving your symptoms.
- Whether beneficial bacteria are present in adequate numbers.
- Your susceptibility to specific systemic diseases.
- The most effective treatment protocols for your unique profile.

Think of it like soil testing before planting a garden.

You wouldn't randomly throw fertilizer at plants and hope for the best — you'd test the soil composition and add exactly what's needed.

***Your oral microbiome deserves the same precision.***

## **How functional dentistry creates lasting change**

Once we have testing data, holistic dental care creates personalized healing protocols that address your whole health:

### **Oral microbiome restoration**

Instead of eliminating all bacteria indiscriminately, we selectively reduce harmful strains while supporting beneficial ones through targeted probiotics and antimicrobial protocols.

### **Nutritional optimization**

Certain nutrient deficiencies directly contribute to oral disease. Vitamin D, K2, and minerals like zinc play crucial roles in maintaining healthy teeth and gums.

### **Biocompatible materials**

We highly recommend eliminating mercury amalgams and using materials that support rather than disrupt your oral ecosystem and overall health.

### **Systemic inflammation reduction**

By healing your mouth, we reduce the inflammatory burden on your entire body.

One patient shared that her canker sores disappeared and gums stopped bleeding within weeks of starting her personalized protocol.

### **Collaborative care**

I work closely with functional medicine practitioners to ensure your oral health optimization supports your broader health goals.

If [gut health issues](/blog/gut-dysbiosis-symptoms) are present alongside oral concerns, I often recommend comprehensive [gut testing](https://theacademy.theurbanmonk.com/a/2148175796/NNzcjENH) alongside oral microbiome testing for a complete picture of your digestive health from mouth to microbiome.

While we offer these tests separately, taking both provides a more holistic understanding of your oral-gut axis.
