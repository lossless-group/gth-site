## The Four Markers Your Doctor Isn't Checking

Standard blood work looks at inflammatory markers like CRP, maybe checks your thyroid, runs a complete metabolic panel. *All useful information*.

***But none of it tells you if your gut barrier has failed.***

Comprehensive leaky gut testing measures four critical markers that reveal exactly what's happening at your intestinal wall:

### The Four Critical Markers Standard Tests Miss

What comprehensive gut barrier testing reveals

🚪

#### Zonulin

Controls intestinal tight junctions — your gut's gatekeepers that regulate what enters your bloodstream

🧱

#### Occludin

Structural protein holding tight junctions together — antibodies signal physical breakdown of gut lining

🦠

#### LPS Antibodies

Proves bacterial endotoxins are escaping your gut — triggers system-wide inflammation

🍄

#### Candida Antibodies

Indicates aggressive yeast overgrowth drilling holes in your intestinal wall

**Zonulin** is the protein that controls your gut's tight junctions — the gatekeepers between your digestive system and bloodstream.

When Zonulin is elevated, those gates are stuck open, allowing undigested food particles, toxins, and bacteria to flood into your circulation.²

Research shows elevated Zonulin predicts autoimmune disease development years before diagnosis.³

**Occludin** is the structural protein that holds tight junctions together. Think of it as the mortar between bricks.

When you see Occludin antibodies in blood, it means your gut lining's physical architecture is breaking down.⁴ This isn't just dysfunction — it's structural collapse.

**LPS (Lipopolysaccharide)** antibodies prove bacterial endotoxins are escaping your gut.

LPS triggers inflammatory cascades associated with cardiovascular disease⁵,¹⁴,¹⁵, type 2 diabetes¹⁶,¹⁷, and Alzheimer's disease¹⁸,⁵.

When these toxins enter your bloodstream, your immune system goes into overdrive, creating system-wide inflammation.

**Candida antibodies** indicate yeast has transformed from its benign form into aggressive fungal threads that literally drill holes in your gut lining.⁶

Candida produces over 70 different toxins, including acetaldehyde — the same compound that causes hangover symptoms.

These markers don't fluctuate day-to-day like some biomarkers. They're stable indicators of ongoing gut barrier damage.⁷ And standard medical testing completely ignores them.

## The Food Sensitivity Connection Nobody Talks About

Here's something that surprises most people: ***elevated gut barrier markers almost always come with multiple*** [***food sensitivities***](https://theurbanmonk.com/food-sensitivities/)***.***

It's not a coincidence.

When your gut barrier breaks down, undigested food proteins leak into your bloodstream.

Your immune system sees these proteins as foreign invaders and creates IgG antibodies against them.⁸

This is completely different from IgE food allergies that cause immediate reactions.

### IgE Allergies vs IgG Sensitivities

Understanding the critical difference

⚡

#### IgE Allergies

⏱️ Timing:

Immediate reaction within minutes

🚨 Symptoms:

Hives, swelling, difficulty breathing, anaphylaxis

🔍 Detection:

Obvious and easy to identify

🕐

#### IgG Sensitivities

⏱️ Timing:

Delayed reaction — hours to days later

🚨 Symptoms:

Brain fog, fatigue, bloating, joint pain, inflammation

🔍 Detection:

Nearly impossible without testing

IgG sensitivities create delayed inflammation — symptoms show up hours or days later, making them nearly impossible to identify without testing.

I've seen patients eliminate gluten and dairy for months with minimal improvement, only to discover through testing they're reacting to eggs, almonds, or chicken — foods they're eating daily as "safe" alternatives.

***You can't eliminate your way to healing when you don't know what you're reacting to.***

Food sensitivity testing using IgG antibody measurement with C3d complement activation shows exactly which foods are triggering inflammation in your body right now.⁹

It's the difference between guessing with generic elimination diets and having actual data.

[Understanding food sensitivities and gut inflammation](/blog/gut-testing-for-inflammation) is essential because continuing to eat your trigger foods while trying to heal your gut is like bailing water from a boat without plugging the hole.
