## **What Leaky Gut Actually Means**

Think of your intestinal lining as a selective security system.

When it's working properly, this barrier allows nutrients to pass through while keeping toxins, undigested food particles, and bacteria out of your bloodstream.

With increased intestinal permeability, the protective barrier weakens.

The tight junctions between intestinal cells — think of them as secure seals — begin to loosen.

When this happens, substances that should stay contained in your gut leak into your bloodstream, triggering an immune response throughout your body.¹
