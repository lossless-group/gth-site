## **What Comprehensive Testing Actually Shows**

When we run complete microbiome testing, we're looking at specific markers that generic stool tests miss:

**Zonulin** levels indicate intestinal permeability.

Zonulin is a physiological modulator of intercellular tight junctions — the protein structures that control what passes through your gut lining.

When zonulin levels are elevated, it signals that tight junctions are opening too wide, allowing undigested food particles and toxins into your bloodstream.⁸

**Secretory IgA** shows your gut immune function. Low levels suggest your gut can't properly defend against pathogens.

**Calprotectin** measures intestinal inflammation. Elevated levels indicate active inflammatory processes damaging your gut lining.
