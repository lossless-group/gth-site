## If you're tired of the same dental problems despite excellent care, precision dentistry finally gives you personalized answers

After 18+ years in clinical dentistry, I've seen the pattern repeat itself: patients come in frustrated because they're doing everything right — brushing twice daily, flossing religiously, seeing their dentist regularly — yet still dealing with bleeding gums, bad breath, or recurring infections.

*"What am I doing wrong?"* they ask.

And honestly? ***Usually, nothing.***

Your mouth contains over 700 distinct bacterial species,¹,² and the composition varies dramatically from person to person — even from tooth to tooth.²

When I practiced traditional dentistry, I relied on visual examination and clinical experience to treat what I could see. And that worked well for many patients.

But for those with chronic issues that wouldn't resolve? I was essentially guessing at which protocol might work.

That's what led me to functional dentistry and precision testing.

Not because traditional dentistry is inadequate, but because ***even the most skilled dentist can't identify bacterial species with the naked eye. ***

We can see inflammation and measure pocket depths, but we can't see which of hundreds of possible bacteria are driving those symptoms.

***Precision dentistry changes that equation. ***

By testing your oral microbiome first, we identify exactly which bacteria are causing problems and design treatment specifically for your mouth — not for the average patient, ***but for you.***

**In this article, you'll learn** why testing reveals what examination can't see, [how bacterial imbalances drive chronic issues](/blog/oral-health-and-chronic-disease), real patient results, and whether this approach fits your situation.

## What Precision Dentistry Actually Means

Think of precision dentistry as adding a microscope to your dentist's toolkit.

Traditional examination — visual inspection, X-rays, clinical experience — remains the foundation. It's excellent at what it does.

Precision dentistry just adds bacterial data that enhances those traditional skills.

Here's what makes it different: ***when we test your oral microbiome, we create a bacterial "fingerprint" unique to your mouth.***

The results show which of 700+ possible species are present, in what quantities, and whether harmful bacteria have overgrown.

This matters because two patients with identical symptoms — say, [bleeding gums](/blog/bleeding-gums) — might have completely different bacterial culprits.

Without testing, we'd treat them the same way. ***With precision dentistry, we target each person's specific imbalances***.

Traditional approaches focus on generic hygiene protocols and reactive symptom treatment.

Precision methods keep all those foundations but add personalized treatment plans based on your actual bacterial data, targeted materials selected for your body, and [progress monitoring](/blog/periodontal-bacteria-levels) with adjustments as your microbiome shifts.

The distinction?

Your dentist has always worked with the best tools available. Testing simply provides information that wasn't accessible before.

## The Science Behind Precision Testing

Every day, you swallow between 150 billion and 1 trillion bacteria from your mouth.³

Your oral microbiome is the second most diverse microbial community in your body, with composition varying tooth to tooth.⁴

Here's why that matters: we can now identify exactly which bacteria are causing problems.

Testing reveals exact pathogen identification *(is Porphyromonas gingivalis or Prevotella intermedia driving your gum disease?)*, bacterial load levels, inflammation markers, and which dental materials work best for you.

The [oral-systemic connection](/blog/oral-systemic-connection) makes this crucial.

For diabetics, the relationship runs both ways: diabetes increases gum disease risk, while active gum disease makes blood sugar harder to control.⁷

When harmful mouth bacteria enter your bloodstream through inflamed gums, they trigger inflammation throughout your body.

##

## Real Transformations Through Testing-First Care

Let me share two cases that show how precision dentistry works when traditional approaches hit their limits.

**Case one:** A patient came to me after three years of bleeding gums despite excellent home care and multiple deep cleanings.

Her dentist was skilled and thorough, but the bleeding wouldn't stop.

Oral microbiome testing revealed significant Prevotella intermedia overgrowth — a specific anaerobic bacterium that standard treatments don't effectively target.

We designed a protocol with targeted probiotics and antimicrobials matching her bacterial profile, plus dietary adjustments to modify her oral pH.

Within ten days, the bleeding stopped completely.

*"My dentist wasn't doing anything wrong,"* she told me. *"We just needed to know what was actually happening in my mouth."*

**Case two:** A Type 2 diabetic struggled with chronic gum disease despite excellent glycemic control and dental care.

His HbA1c stayed stubbornly elevated. Testing identified bacterial overgrowth directly interfering with his metabolic health.

We implemented targeted periodontal therapy while coordinating with his endocrinologist.

Within three months, his HbA1c dropped from 7.8% to 6.9%, and his gum pockets decreased from 5-6mm to 2-3mm.

*"I didn't realize my mouth was affecting my diabetes this much,"* he said. *"Getting treatment for my actual bacterial problem changed everything."*

Both patients received good traditional care. Testing provided the missing piece — specific bacterial data that generic approaches couldn't address.

## The Five Pillars of Precision Dentistry

**Comprehensive Testing** forms the foundation.

Oral microbiome testing reveals which of 700+ bacterial species are present and in what quantities, uncovering invisible imbalances that visual examination misses.

**Personalized Protocols** follow testing.

Instead of generic recommendations, we create custom plans based on YOUR bacterial profile — targeting specific pathogens with tailored antimicrobials, specific probiotics, and customized hygiene approaches.

**Systemic Health Integration** recognizes mouth-body connections.

We coordinate with medical providers when needed for conditions like diabetes or [heart disease](/blog/oral-health-and-heart-disease).

Periodontal disease increases risk of preterm birth and low birth weight,⁸ [affects gut health](/blog/oral-bacteria-and-gut-health), blood sugar control in diabetics,⁷ increases coronary heart disease risk,⁵ and has been linked to Alzheimer's disease.⁶

**Biocompatible Materials** ensure treatments work with your body.

Testing identifies which materials YOUR body tolerates best — particularly important for restorations, implants, and periodontal work.

**Ongoing Monitoring** maintains results.

Regular retesting tracks how your microbiome responds, allowing protocol adjustments for sustained success.

## Is Precision Dentistry Right for You?

Consider this approach if you're dealing with chronic gum disease unresponsive to traditional treatment, recurrent infections despite excellent home care, persistent bad breath, [systemic conditions like diabetes or heart disease](/blog/oral-systemic-connection), pregnancy planning (periodontal disease increases risk of preterm birth and low birth weight⁸), or you want proactive, data-driven care.

The ideal candidate?

Anyone frustrated with trial-and-error approaches or seeking treatment for root causes rather than symptom management.

Precision dentistry works alongside your current dentist's expertise.

Ask your provider about [oral microbiome testing](/blog/oral-microbiome-testing). If unavailable, seeking a [functional or holistic dentist](/blog/holistic-dental-care) trained in precision methods might be your next step.
