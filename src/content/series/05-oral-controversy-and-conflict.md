Every field has questions its practitioners argue about in private and avoid in
public. This episode takes those questions on directly, and it does not pretend
the arguments are settled.

Fillings, and what the materials debate actually rests on. Old root canals, and
what to do if you suspect one is harboring infection years after it was
declared finished. Implants, and what genuinely matters in choosing one.

The tone here is deliberately careful. Where the evidence is strong, Elmira and
Pedram say so. Where it is contested, they say that too, and explain what a
reasonable person might do while the field works it out.
