A large number of people avoid dental care entirely because of fear, and that
avoidance has real consequences that compound quietly for decades. This episode
is aimed squarely at them.

Much of what created the fear belongs to a version of dentistry that is on its
way out. Lasers that treat without penetrating tissue. Approaches built around
preserving teeth rather than removing them. Procedures designed with the
patient's nervous system in mind rather than only the tooth.

The back half turns forward. Elmira, Pedram and Lora lay out where oral health
is heading, and what it means for the ordinary daily decisions, what you eat,
how you brush, whether flossing is the right tool, that most of us made once and
never revisited.
