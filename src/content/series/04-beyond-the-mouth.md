This is the episode the whole series was building toward. Everything so far has
stayed inside the mouth. Here it leaves.

Pedram and Elmira trace the routes: down, by swallowing, into a gut that is the
same continuous tube; and across, through inflamed gum tissue into the
bloodstream and from there anywhere blood goes. That includes a pregnancy. It
includes the brain.

They also tell the historical story that explains why this is news to so many
people. Dentistry and medicine were separated roughly 150 years ago, and the gap
between them has been quietly expensive for patients ever since.
