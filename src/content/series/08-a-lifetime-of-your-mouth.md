A bonus episode, and a quieter one. The series has spent seven episodes on
mechanism. This one takes the long view.

An ageing mouth has its own requirements. Saliva production tends to drop,
often because of medications rather than age itself. Gums recede and expose root
surfaces that behave differently than enamel. Decades of dental work need
maintaining alongside the teeth around it.

Elmira and Pedram make the case that we are living through something like a
golden age for this work, with tools and knowledge that were unavailable a
generation ago. And they make the point worth ending on: the mouth responds to
care at any age, and the value of starting now does not decline with how long
you waited.
