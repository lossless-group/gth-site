Climate is the right metaphor. Your mouth is an environment with a chemistry,
a weather pattern and a population, and small persistent changes to the
conditions decide which species thrive there.

This episode follows the mechanics. How certain bacteria attach to enamel and
secrete the acid that dissolves it. Why saliva matters more than almost anyone
realizes, and where most of it actually comes from. How stress, which feels like
a purely mental event, ends up showing as decay.

It also takes the first serious step outward: the two-way relationship between
gum disease and Type 2 diabetes, and the way the shape of a jaw can turn into a
sleep problem with consequences well beyond the mouth.
