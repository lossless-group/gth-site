We start where nobody starts, which is with the idea that your mouth is part of
your body. It sounds obvious written down. It is not how medicine is organized,
and it is not how most of us were taught to think about brushing.

This first episode lays out the case. Cavities are not simply the result of
sugar and poor discipline. Bleeding gums are not a minor annoyance you mention
at your cleaning. Both are signals from a bacterial community that has shifted,
and that community does not stay in your mouth.

Elmira and Pedram walk through what changed in the last century of eating, why
crowded teeth are a relatively recent human problem, and what it would mean to
treat the mouth as the beginning of the digestive tract rather than a separate
department.
