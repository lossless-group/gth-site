The instinct, once you learn there are harmful bacteria in your mouth, is to
kill them. This episode is about why that instinct backfires.

A strong antiseptic rinse does not discriminate. It clears the room, and the
species that refill an empty room fastest are rarely the ones you wanted. The
better strategy is crowding out: make conditions favor the organisms that keep
your mouth stable, and let them hold the territory.

Elmira and hygienist Lora Hooper get specific about pH, about what receding gums
are actually telling you, and about why plaque, of all things, is where a lot of
the interesting research is happening right now.
